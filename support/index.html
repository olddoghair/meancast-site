How does MeanCast work?

It takes six global weather models (GFS, ECMWF, ICON, GEM, JMA and UKMO) plus the National Weather Service and averages them into one forecast. Then it shows how much they agree, so you know how much to trust it. The grumpier the cloud, the more the forecasts disagree.

What does the rain percentage mean?

It's the share of the forecasts that expect rain. 4 out of 7 is about 57%.

Why isn't the National Weather Service showing?

The NWS only covers the United States. Outside the U.S., MeanCast averages the six models. If a forecast didn't load, Settings › Forecasts to average shows which one and why.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: the average, agreement, a 24-hour hourly forecast, one place, feels like, frost line, "Was it right?" and the small widget.

Pro ($1.99 a month or $9.99 a year) adds every individual forecast, unlimited places, a 48-hour hourly forecast, rain and disagreement alerts, best time outside, and the medium and lock-screen widgets.

Everyone gets 30 days of Pro free when they first open the app. After that it simply switches to the free version. You're never charged unless you choose to subscribe.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Open MeanCast, tap the gear for Settings, tap MeanCast Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then MeanCast, and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I'm not getting alerts

Check that alerts are on in MeanCast's Settings, and that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app › Notifications › MeanCast. Alerts are for the place you're viewing, and rain alerts come about an hour before most forecasts expect rain. Disagreement alerts come at 7 PM, only when tomorrow's forecasts really split.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for MeanCast. For the lock screen, touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and tap the widget area.

Does MeanCast track me?

No. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your location is only used to get your forecast. Read the privacy policy.
