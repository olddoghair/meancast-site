Privacy Policy

The short version: MeanCast doesn't collect, store or sell any information about you.

Effective October 18, 2026

Location

If you allow it, MeanCast uses your iPhone's location to show the forecast where you are. The location is used only at that moment. It is sent, without your name or any identifier, to the weather services listed below so they can return a forecast for that spot.

MeanCast does not store your location on any server, keep a location history, or share it with anyone else. If you don't allow location access, MeanCast shows the forecast for New York City or a place you choose.

Weather services

To get forecasts, the app sends a place's coordinates to:

  • The MeanCast forecast server, which we run on Cloudflare. It rounds the coordinates to a square about 1.4 miles across and gets the model forecasts for that square from Open-Meteo, and, in the U.S., recent readings from the nearest NOAA weather station to fine-tune them. It saves the forecast for that square for up to an hour so nearby people can share it. It doesn't keep a record of who asked or exactly where.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), for global weather model forecasts, only if the MeanCast server can't be reached
  • The U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov), for its forecast and current conditions, in the U.S. only

These services receive the request the way any website receives a visit, and their own privacy policies apply to it. Cloudflare's policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Alerts

If you turn on rain or disagreement alerts, MeanCast checks the forecast in the background for the place you're viewing, using the same weather services, and shows a notification on your iPhone. Alerts are created on your device. We don't run a notification server and never see them.

Settings and saved places

Your preferences, such as °F or °C, which forecasts to average, and the places you save, are kept only on your iPhone so the app and its widgets can use them. They are never sent to us.

MeanCast Pro and the free trial

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment and never shares your payment details or Apple Account with us. The app only learns whether Pro is active.

To run the 30-day free trial, MeanCast saves the date you first opened it in your iPhone's Keychain, so reinstalling doesn't restart the trial. That date stays on your device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No advertising or ad tracking, ever
  • No analytics that identify you
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Crash reports and feedback

If you've agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. If you test MeanCast through TestFlight, Apple may also share feedback you choose to send. We use these only to fix problems. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Children

MeanCast is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
