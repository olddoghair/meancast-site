How accurate is MeanCast?

Every morning we log the forecast for Central Park in New York, then score it against what the official weather station recorded. This page updates on its own, good days and bad.

Last 30 days, average miss

    Average miss is how far the forecast high and low were from what happened, in °F. Shorter is better. The small number is how often the high or low was off by 4°F or more. Everyone is scored on the same days.

    Who's on the board

    MeanCast is the number the app shows: six weather models plus the National Weather Service, averaged and fine-tuned with nearby weather stations. The six models are listed on their own so you can see how the average compares with each one.
